Wellness Tips 🌿

Simple indoor and outdoor gardening tips to support your plants and your well-being.

Indoor Gardening

Place indoor plants near soft natural light and rotate them weekly for even growth.

Outdoor Gardening

Water outdoor plants early in the morning to reduce evaporation and protect leaves.

Wellness Habit

Spend five quiet minutes near your plants daily to refresh your mood and reduce stress.

Watering Care

Check soil moisture before watering. Too much water may damage plant roots.

Plant Health

Inspect leaves regularly for pests, yellow spots, or dryness to prevent bigger issues.

Beginner Tip

Start with easy-care plants like Snake Plant, Aloe Vera, Pothos, or Peace Lily.
